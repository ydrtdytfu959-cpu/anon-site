## Scope and release distinction

These corrections exist in source and pass local automated tests. They do not deploy the staged detail/commerce routes, prove production Cognito/DynamoDB behavior, authorize merchant purchasing, or activate payment. Saved selections remain browser-local device data shared across accounts on that browser; protected quotes/orders are invalidated on account change/logout, and explicit browser-data deletion erases the saved selections. An approved customer-data/cart retention policy remains a release decision; a device cart is never evidence of ownership of an order.

Price-change acknowledgement cannot authorize a purchase in this release: all checkout/payment actions remain disabled. A future operational purchasing path must obtain fresh server authority and bind explicit customer acceptance to its final payable quote.
